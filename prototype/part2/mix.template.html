THE MEASUREMENT

The keys present in all twelve runs are exactly the eleven the article's get_environ() sets. Not a subset, not a superset. The article's list is precisely the floor — what every server provides and every framework may rely on. Gunicorn and Waitress each add thirteen more, and not the same thirteen.

DEPARTURE — BEYOND THE ARTICLE

The article names Gunicorn and Waitress but never runs them; it only ever runs its own webserver2.py. Both were installed and really run here against the article's own unmodified application files, so the claim it makes in words could be checked. Under ADR 0002: checked against the article's own list of servers, real, and marked here. The Django project is ours too — the article says to clone its repository rather than printing it, so its /hello view is authored by us.
