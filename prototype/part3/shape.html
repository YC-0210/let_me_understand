One more shape

The drawing has eight marks and none of them is a container. Four candidates for a ninth, each one shape with one meaning, each shown on the same three moments. Pick one or none. Every number drawn below was really captured.

1 · The fork

Should be obvious: the child is a copy of the parent, and both of them are now holding the same connection.

2 · Two people waiting

Should be obvious: clients 2 and 3 are connected and simply stuck, because the one server is busy with client 1. Nothing is broken — they are in a queue.

3 · Everything the article warns you about, at once

Should be obvious: the parent is holding four things it will never give back, three children are dead but not gone, and all three clients are waiting for a close that is never coming.

Real values: webserver3b with 3 clients takes 1502 ms; webserver3c takes 503 ms; webserver3d ends holding 4 descriptors with 3 zombies and 0 of 3 clients released; the article's REQUEST_QUEUE_SIZE is 5. All from fork.data.json.
