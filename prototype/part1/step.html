Stepping Down Three Ways

Same six real answers, same two rungs. Only the way you move between one page name and all of them changes. Pick whichever makes the relationship obvious.

A

One window multiplies

The window you are looking at splits into six and gathers back into one. The selected window never leaves the screen, so you can see the six are the same thing at six settings.

B

Both at once, side by side

No transition at all — the single case and the whole set share the screen. Hover a row to try it without committing; click to keep it.

C

The address bar grows

The abstraction lives inside the control you were already using. The address bar stops holding one address and holds all six, each with its answer. The page below still shows one.
