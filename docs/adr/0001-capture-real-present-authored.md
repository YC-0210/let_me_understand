# Capture real, present authored

Everything the Visualization shows is produced by really running the system and recording
what happened; nothing is a plausible-looking mock. But the drawing is authored against
that recording rather than being a dump of it, so the picture can be clear without being
invented. The seam between the two is the **Model**: capture backs the Model's claims as
**Witnesses**, and every rendered pixel is a function of the Model.

## Consequences

Unplug the capture and the Visualization still renders — but its claims fall back to
**Citations** only, and unsupported claims must be marked as such rather than quietly
drawn. This is what keeps the tool honest when it meets a Source whose system cannot be
executed, which most Sources will be.
