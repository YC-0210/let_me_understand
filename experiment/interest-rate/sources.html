Sources & scope

An original, interactive experiment inspired by 小Lin说’s teaching sequence. All numerical examples are illustrative. The lesson covers the opening through 8:17, before the later asset-market discussion.

The teaching reference

小Lin说 · 关于利率，你需要知道的那些事儿. The supplied transcript was read through the cutoff. Selected video sequences and diagram transitions were inspected directly in the player; this is not a claim of uninterrupted viewing.

  • 0:46–1:02: one concrete amount, two dates, then a name for the relationship.
  • 1:04–3:24: a deliberately simplified world, a spending chain, then the constraint that makes the first story incomplete.
  • 3:38–4:40: take one loan quote apart before introducing benchmarks.
  • 4:45–6:11: one benchmark becomes a set of maturities and a curve.
  • 6:14–8:13: use “market or central bank?” as a puzzle, then separate short-rate steering from longer-rate influence.

Accuracy checks

  • Bank of England: how monetary policy transmits — the demand channel has delays and depends on conditions.
  • Federal Reserve: yield curve models — distinguish maturity from calendar time and expected short rates from term premiums.
  • Bank of England: how QE works — purchases, reserves, yields and limits.

Deliberate clarifications

Interest and an interest rate are different quantities. A low rate does not guarantee inflation or prosperity. “Risk-free” is a limited benchmark convention. Overnight rates are normally annualized. Central-bank signals can strongly influence longer rates through expectations, even when they do not directly set them.

The loan-price breakdown is simplified. The one-year bond has just one payment, so its yield is easy to calculate. Historical Japan/2022 policy claims are not presented as current facts. Material after 8:17—stocks, housing, gold, exchange rates—is excluded.

Visual approach

Stable amounts and anchors, progressive diagram construction, and visible exchanges adapt the source’s explanatory logic. These are original drawings, not video screenshots or copied artwork.
